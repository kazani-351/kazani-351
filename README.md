### Hi, I'm kazani.

I build small AI tools, ship them live, and then write about how they work.
Most of what I make is for people who get left out of tech: older relatives, blind and
low-vision neighbours, district health officers.

**Shipped**

- **[Jan Swasth Map](https://github.com/kazani-351/jan-swasth-map)**: forecasts seasonal dengue and diarrhoea risk for India's districts and drafts a response plan with an AI agent. [Live](https://jan-swasth-map.kazani.workers.dev)
- **[Baton](https://github.com/kazani-351/baton-webmcp)**: a WebMCP store where an AI agent proposes each action and you confirm it before anything happens.
- **[Good Neighbor Agent](https://github.com/kazani-351/good-neighbor-agent)**: triages help requests from blind and low-vision neighbours, answering, routing to a volunteer, or escalating to a human.
- **[Samjha Do](https://github.com/kazani-351/samjha-do)**: describe a confusing phone screen and get a calm explanation in Hindi or Marathi, as text and audio.
- **[Handoff](https://github.com/kazani-351/handoff)**: show someone one photo on your phone without them swiping through the rest. [Live](https://handoff.kazani.workers.dev)
- **[system-upgrade](https://github.com/kazani-351/system-upgrade)**: an agent skill that audits your own AI setup.
